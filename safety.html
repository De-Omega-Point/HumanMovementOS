Training safety

Human Movement OS is a coaching and exercise-management tool. It is not medical diagnosis or medical clearance.

Use movements you can control, use easier variations when needed, and stop for new or increasing pain. Advanced skills, impact, sprinting and inversion should be progressed deliberately.

For a current injury, unexplained symptoms, known osteoporosis or another relevant condition, obtain appropriate professional assessment before heavy loading, impact or inversion. Chest pain, fainting or severe unusual breathlessness require urgent medical attention.
