Privacy notice

Human Movement OS is designed to minimise personal data. A connected coaching account may store identity, programme assignments, workout results, adherence, onboarding responses, trainer notes and billing status.

Payment-card details are handled by Stripe and are not intended to be stored by Human Movement OS. Access is role-based: Clients see their own information, Trainers see assigned Clients, and Administrators manage platform operations.

Local backups can contain personal training information. Store them securely. For production deployment, the operator must publish contact, retention, access, correction and deletion procedures appropriate to the jurisdiction.
